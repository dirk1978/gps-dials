      Map settings

      Everything works without an account. OpenStreetMap appears in your overlays for free, and the Sync tab's Satellite and Hybrid views are just for lining up clips.

      Richer maps with Mapbox (optional)

      A free Mapbox token adds satellite, hybrid, outdoors, dark and light maps to your overlays. They're polished and easy to read on video, and the satellite view is lovely for rides and flights.

      What it costs

      For most people, nothing. Mapbox has a generous free allowance, last published as 200,000 map images a month, then about $0.50 per 1,000 after that. One clip needs a few dozen images at most, and GPS Dials keeps each one on your PC, so reusing it costs nothing. As a rough guide, a full day's ride uses up to a thousand or so, so you'd need to make a great many overlays to pay anything. Prices can change, so check Mapbox's pricing page.

      How to get a token

      1. Create an account at mapbox.com. They may ask for a card, even for the free allowance.
      2. Open your Access tokens page.
      3. Copy your default public token, which starts with pk., and paste it below.

      Stored encrypted on this PC and sent only to Mapbox. Then choose a Mapbox style for any map in the Design tab. Check their terms before publishing their maps in videos.
